- Compensating controls and verification:
- Alternatives considered:
- Authorized decision-maker and date:

- Remediation owner, next action and tracking link:
- Closure criteria and eventual evidence:

Exceptions do not erase findings. Architectural “grandfathering” is not security risk acceptance. Expired exceptions return to owner review; tools must not silently renew them. Do not copy acceptance across services without checking applicability.
